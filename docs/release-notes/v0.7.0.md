## What's new

- **Jellyfin** (Settings → Jellyfin, in purple): Resume Relay finds your Jellyfin server on the network, and you sign
  in as a Jellyfin administrator with a user name and password, Quick Connect, or an API key. Map the folders (Plex's
  are suggested when it runs on the same computer) and add users. Each Jellyfin user becomes a player:
  - A stop on a Zidoo, in Plex or in Jellyfin is copied to every other player, to the second; finishing marks it watched.
  - Progress Reset, Watch Sync, Reset TV Show and the watched-marks check include Jellyfin users.
  - Live Poster shows what a Jellyfin user is playing (with Jellyfin's poster when the folder has none), and Recently
    Watched includes their stops. Highly Acclaimed uses Jellyfin's rating only for titles neither Plex nor a Zidoo
    rates, and labels it as Jellyfin's. Jellyfin's critic rating (Rotten Tomatoes) is never used.
  - A Jellyfin activity badge you can replace with your own picture (Settings → App → Activity badges).
  - Needs Jellyfin 10.9 or newer for best results.
- **Feature Presentation** (Live Poster), a new slideshow set with its own marquee background: each screen picks one
  title of its own with **Select a title** on its card. The list has every movie and TV show your libraries know (Plex,
  Jellyfin, a Zidoo's poster wall), each once: no special features, no episodes. Search it, pick one, and for a TV
  show its season (the season's poster is used). It shows once per round; turn the screen's other sets off to show
  only it. Something playing still takes over with Now Showing.
- The dashboard has Resume Relay's icon, in the browser tab and at the top of the page. The Live Poster tab shows the
  screen apps' own icon.
- **Join our Discord** (Settings → App): news, help and ideas from other Resume Relay users.
- New releases are announced on Resume Relay's Discord.
- Fixed: Jellyfin users showed the Zidoo badge in a Live Poster screen card's player list.
- The README now opens with Resume Relay's picture.
