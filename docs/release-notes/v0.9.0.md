## What's new

- **Apple TV** (Live Poster only, like a Kaleidescape): **Scan network** finds Apple TVs. Pair one once from its card
  on the Players tab (press **Pair** and type the PIN the TV shows), and a Live Poster screen can follow it.
  - **The Plex app:** Resume Relay asks the Plex server what that Apple TV is playing (matched by its address) and
    uses Plex's poster, or your folder's. Plex only needs to be signed in on the Plex card; no Plex user has to be added.
  - **Any other app** (Netflix, Disney+, Hulu, the Apple TV app…): the title the app reports, with a poster from TMDB.
    Of the title's posters (2:3 only), the best: in the title's language first, then the biggest (2000×3000, then
    1920×2880, then 1000×1500…), the most voted breaking ties; for an episode, its season's poster. When TMDB has
    none, the app's own artwork.
  - Its card shows what's playing and in which app. Nothing is copied to or from it: an app on an Apple TV plays its
    own streams. Watching for a minute or more is noted on the Activity tab.
  - Built without an Apple TV to test on, on the Apple TV's own (unpublished) remote protocol through
    AppleTvControlLibrary (MIT), a port of pyatv. If yours doesn't pair or misbehaves, please say so on Discord.
- **Settings → App → About:** where Resume Relay's parts come from, including "This product uses the TMDB API but is not
  endorsed or certified by TMDB."
- Activity badges: the Apple TV badge's picture can be set too.
