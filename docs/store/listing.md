# Store listing text (screen apps)

Use the same text in every store. **No third-party brand names anywhere in the listing** (titles, descriptions,
bullets, keywords): Amazon rejected 0.6.2 on 2026-11-03 for "Zidoo" and "Plex" in the keywords and description, as
third-party IP. The apps themselves name neither. Pictures are in this folder (made by tools/make_store_assets.py).

**App name:** Resume Relay Live Poster

**Short description (80 characters max):**
A digital movie poster for your home theater: what's playing, in 4K, on any TV.

**Full description:**
Turn any TV into a digital movie poster for your home theater.

Resume Relay Live Poster shows the poster of what's playing in your theater, in 4K. When nothing is playing, it shows a
slideshow of posters you add as Coming Soon, titles recently watched, and highly acclaimed movies and series from your
library, each dissolving into the next under classic marquee headers. Hang the TV on its side and the poster fills
the screen.

How it works: Resume Relay, a free program, runs on a computer in your home (Windows, Ubuntu, or Docker on a NAS). It
follows your home theater's media players and media server, makes each poster into a 4K video, and sends it to this app. Install it from
github.com/ckenisell/resume-relay, then add this screen with the 4-digit code it shows.

- Finds Resume Relay on your network by itself
- 4K posters, for TVs standing or hung on their side
- Keeps the screen awake
- No accounts, no ads, nothing collected
- Free, with a demo you can try right away

**Keywords:** movie poster, home theater, now playing, digital poster, cinema, marquee, 4K, slideshow, poster frame

**Category:** Entertainment (Roku: Personalization or Utilities if offered). **Price:** free. **Ads:** none.

**Content rating answers:** no violence, sexual content, language, drugs, gambling, user-generated content, sharing of
location or personal data. Everyone / all ages.

**Privacy policy URL:** https://github.com/ckenisell/resume-relay/blob/main/docs/privacy.md

**Notes for the reviewer:**
This app shows posters from Resume Relay, a free program the user runs on a computer at home; without it, the app
looks for it on the network and then asks for its address. To see the app working without Resume Relay, choose
**Try a demo** on that screen (or press OK / Select on the remote and choose Try a demo). The demo plays a slideshow of
invented posters, 8 seconds each. Press OK for options, and choose Stop the demo to leave it.
