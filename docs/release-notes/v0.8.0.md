## What's new

- **Kaleidescape** (Live Poster only): Scan network finds Kaleidescape players (or add one by its IP address), and a
  Live Poster screen can follow one. When it plays a movie, the screen shows Now Showing with the Kaleidescape's own
  cover. Its card on the Players tab (in blue-grey) shows the movie and where it is.
  - A Kaleidescape plays its own copies of movies, not your files, and can't be given a resume point, so nothing is
    copied to or from it. It isn't on Progress Reset or Watch Sync.
  - Its stops (after at least a minute) are noted on the Activity tab and go into Recently Watched.
  - The movies Resume Relay has seen it play can be a screen's Feature Presentation. (A Kaleidescape can't list its
    whole library, and keeps no review scores, so it doesn't add to Highly Acclaimed.)
  - Its own activity badge picture can be set in Settings → App → Activity badges.
  - Built from Kaleidescape's published control protocol without a player to test on. If yours doesn't show up or
    misbehaves, please say so on Discord.
- Progress Reset and Watch Sync are greyed out until a Zidoo, Plex or Jellyfin player is added; Live Poster until any
  player is.
- Zidoo player cards show the Zidoo badge next to the power button, like the Plex and Jellyfin cards.
- Fixed: with Copying off (watch-only), the watched-marks check still copied watched marks between players. It now
  only notes on the Activity tab what it would copy, like stop copying does.
- Roku app: tells the Roku when its first screen is ready (needed for the Roku Channel Store).
